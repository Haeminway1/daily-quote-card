About Daily Quote Card

Twelve original reflections written for this app. Saved cards and notes are stored only in this browser.

Notifications here are an immediate, optional test. Daily or background push reminders are not enabled. On iPhone/iPad, notification support requires iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later and a Home Screen installation; device settings still apply.

Cards can be read offline after your first online visit. Clearing browser storage removes saved notes.

Add Daily Quote Card to your Home Screen

On iPhone/iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On other browsers, look for Install app in the browser menu. Availability depends on your browser.
